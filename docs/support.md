# BayCue: Parking Spot Keeper Support

BayCue is an offline iPhone parking notebook from Chhunsour Seng. Save a building, floor, photo, and landmark note before you walk away.

## Save a spot

1. In Places, add a building and its floor labels, such as B2, B1, G, 1, and 2.
2. Choose the building on Parking and tap a floor to save immediately.
3. Optionally attach a photo of a pillar, bay number, or entrance, and a landmark note.
4. Tap Finish parking when you leave. Your latest 30 memories remain in History.

Use Just this visit for an occasional stop. For outdoor parking, leave floor labels empty and save the spot.

## Widgets

Long-press the iPhone Home Screen, choose Edit, then Add Widget. Search for BayCue. Lock Screen widgets can be added through Customize on the Lock Screen. Open the app once before adding widgets. If a widget looks out of date, open the app and check your selected building and current memory, then remove and add the widget again. Older sessions are labeled Previous.

## Photos and permissions

Camera access is optional and is requested when you choose to take a photo. The system photo picker lets you choose an existing image. Camera permission can be changed in iPhone Settings.

[Report a problem or request help](https://github.com/Chhunsour/MotoFloor/issues/new). Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that caused the problem. Issues are public, so omit private parking locations, photos, and sensitive information.
