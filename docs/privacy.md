# BayCue Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

BayCue, listed on the App Store as BayCue: Parking Spot Keeper, is provided by Chhunsour Seng.

## Data collection

BayCue does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or developer-operated backend. It does not request location access or record GPS coordinates.

## Local storage

The app stores the building names and floor labels you enter, parking timestamps, landmark notes, preferences, photos you attach, and up to 30 recent parking memories. The app and its widgets share parking information through an Apple App Group on your device.

## Photos and camera

Camera access is optional and is used when you choose to take a parking photo. The system photo picker provides only the image you select. Attached images are resized and re-encoded as JPEG without the original image metadata. Unreferenced app-managed photos are removed when no retained memory uses them.

## Retention and deletion

Recent history is limited to 30 memories. You can remove memories and saved buildings through the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Apple's device backups may include app data according to your device and iCloud backup settings. The developer cannot access or recover your local data remotely.

## Apple services

Apple handles App Store purchases and may provide the developer with sales reports or diagnostic information according to your Apple settings. BayCue does not receive or store payment card information. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policies.

## Support and policy updates

Information you choose to send to support is handled by that support channel. GitHub issues are public and subject to GitHub's policies. Omit private parking locations, photos, and sensitive information.

For privacy questions, [contact support](https://github.com/Chhunsour/MotoFloor/issues/new). Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised effective date.
